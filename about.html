About this project

The AI-Based Student Feedback Analysis & Sentiment Monitoring Portal is a Web Technology course project. It lets students send feedback and helps administrators understand the overall mood of the campus.

How sentiment is calculated

Each comment goes through a small, local routine. TextBlob measures how positive or negative the wording is, and a short list of campus-specific words (such as outdated or helpful) adjusts the result. The final score runs from -1 to +1: 0.10 or higher is Positive, -0.10 or lower is Negative, and anything between is Neutral.

This is a lightweight sentiment-analysis feature, not a highly accurate AI model. It can misjudge sarcasm, mixed opinions and very short comments.

Technology

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js
  • Backend: Python and Flask
  • Database: SQLite through SQLAlchemy
  • Sentiment: TextBlob with a keyword list, running fully on localhost

Privacy

Passwords are stored as hashes. Feedback marked anonymous never shows the student's name or ID to administrators or in reports.
