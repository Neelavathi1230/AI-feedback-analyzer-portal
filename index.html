AI-Based Student Feedback Analysis & Sentiment Monitoring Portal

Students share honest feedback about teaching, courses and campus facilities. The portal reads every comment, labels it Positive, Neutral or Negative, and shows administrators what needs attention.

Submit Feedback Login

Sentiment is detected with a lightweight keyword + TextBlob method. It is a helpful guide, not a perfect judge.

Example comments, as the portal reads them
Positive “The faculty explained everything clearly.”
Neutral “The course was okay.”
Negative “The lab equipment is old and needs improvement.”
